# 🗺️ Empathy Map Canvas

**Project Name:** ShopEEZ — Full Stack MERN E-Commerce Platform
**Date:** June 2026

---

## Persona 1 — The Customer (Rahul, 24, College Student)

> *"I want to shop quickly from my phone without the app freezing or losing my cart."*

| Quadrant | Details |
|:--|:--|
| **Thinks & Feels** | Wants a smooth, premium feel. Worried about credit card safety. Excited when filters narrow results instantly. |
| **Sees** | Clean product cards with ₹ prices, high-res images, brand tags, and star ratings on a dark theme. |
| **Says & Does** | Searches for specific products, filters by price, adds items to wishlist, reviews cart before checkout. |
| **Hears** | Friends say "ShopEEZ has a great UI and fast checkout." Reads online reviews praising the seamless experience. |
| **Pains** | Cart disappears on refresh. Checkout takes too many steps. Unclear stock availability. |
| **Gains** | Persistent cart across sessions. Quick 2-step checkout. Real-time stock indicators on product pages. |

---

## Persona 2 — The Store Administrator (Sarah, 32, Business Owner)

> *"I need to update inventory and track orders without learning complex software."*

| Quadrant | Details |
|:--|:--|
| **Thinks & Feels** | Feels overwhelmed by overly technical admin panels. Wants instant product updates. Anxious when orders pile up. |
| **Sees** | Admin Dashboard with stats cards: Total Products, Total Orders, Revenue (₹), User Count. Low stock alerts. |
| **Says & Does** | Adds new products via a form, edits prices, changes order statuses, reviews recent transactions. |
| **Hears** | "Your product page is outdated." "Customers are asking about order status." |
| **Pains** | Cannot update catalog without developer help. No single view for pending orders. |
| **Gains** | Full-featured `/admin/dashboard` with direct CRUD, order status toggles, and user management. |
